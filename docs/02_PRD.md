# 02 — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Target Audience
- **Tech Recruiters & Talent Acquisition Specialists** seeking motivated, skilled Full-Stack / Software Developers.
- **Engineering Managers & Technical Leads** reviewing code architecture, technology choices, and project depth.
- **Startup Founders & Clients** looking for end-to-end web, mobile, and AI application development.

## Core Features & Requirements
1. **Pill Navigation**: Sticky floating dark pill navbar with brand logo, smooth section jump, active tab indicator, and mobile drawer.
2. **Hero Section (16:9 Composition)**:
   - Greeting badge ("Hello! 👋").
   - Bold headline ("I'm **Hidayathullah**, Software Developer").
   - Supporting narrative on modern web, mobile, and AI solutions.
   - Real photo in professional suit with signature orange circle arch backdrop.
   - 4 compact floating info cards (*Software Developer*, *2+ Years of Learning*, *10+ Projects Completed*, *Always Learning*).
   - "View My Work ↗" and "Download Resume" pill action buttons.
3. **Services Section**: Dark background container (`#080808`) with 6 full-stack & AI service cards.
4. **About Section**: Authentic personal story, CSE 2026 graduation focus, and visual journey timeline (2022–Present).
5. **Skills Section**: Categorized technical competencies with hands-on experience indicators.
6. **Projects Showcase**: 7 data-driven projects with category filters (All, Web, Mobile, AI, Enterprise, Game) and deep-dive Case Study detail modals.
7. **Certifications**: Verified credentials with view modal.
8. **Resume & Contact**: Working resume PDF download and validated contact form.
